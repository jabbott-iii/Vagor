# Vagor Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Vagor is a vacation planner made by Joseph Anthony Abbott III. This policy explains how Vagor
handles your information. The same text is in the app under Settings > Privacy Policy.

## Information Vagor stores

Vagor stores the vacations and events you enter (titles, details, dates, times and time zones) and
your dark mode setting. They are stored only on your device, in Vagor's private app storage.

## Information Vagor collects or shares

None. Vagor has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
Nothing you enter is sent to the developer, and it goes to another app only when you share it or
add it to a calendar, as described below.

## Sharing

When you tap Share Vacation or Share Event, Vagor opens Android's share menu with that vacation or
event as text. The text goes only to the app you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies to it.

When you tap Add to Calendar, Vagor opens your calendar app (Google Calendar, if it's installed)
with that vacation or event filled in: its title, dates, time and time zone, details and vacation,
and for a vacation the list of its events. Vagor doesn't add anything to your calendar itself and
can't read your calendar. The calendar app decides whether and where to save it, and if it's saved
to an account such as your Google Account, it may sync to that account and your other devices. That
app's privacy policy applies to it; for Google Calendar, that's Google's Privacy Policy.

## Reminders

Vagor uses notifications and alarms on your device to remind you on the day a vacation starts or
ends and an hour before each event. Reminders are scheduled on your device, and nothing is sent over
the internet.

## Backup

Vagor turns off Android cloud backup, so your trips aren't copied to Google Drive. If you move to a
new phone with a direct device-to-device transfer, Android may copy Vagor's data to the new phone.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it. Delete a vacation or event in the app,
or uninstall Vagor or clear its storage in Android Settings to remove all of it.

## Security

Android keeps Vagor's data in the app's private storage, which other apps can't access.

## Children

Vagor doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be included in an app update and posted where this
policy is published, with a new effective date.
